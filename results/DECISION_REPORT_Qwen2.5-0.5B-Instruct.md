## Decision 1: two-tier vs three-tier (INT2)

Rule (fixed before the full run): (1) usability gate: only variants at least as accurate as uniform INT4 on BOTH PPL and KL are considered; (2) among those, the INT2 tier is worth keeping only if at least one three-tier variant is on BOTH Pareto frontiers (bits vs KL and bits vs PPL), i.e. no other usable variant is at least as cheap and at least as accurate.

**Result: no three-tier variant is on both frontiers. Every INT2 configuration is beaten by a variant that uses no INT2 at equal or fewer bits. Use two-tier INT8/INT4; report INT2 as a negative ablation.**

## Limitations of this test

- The cache is dequantized to FP16 for each attention call, so peak VRAM is not reduced; only the bytes stored between steps are.
- Quality is measured on WikiText-2 language modelling only; long-context retrieval (passkey) is not covered by this test.
- 20 windows of 576 tokens; conclusions hold for this context length.
